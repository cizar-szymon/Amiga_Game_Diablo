DiabloWarp - release history
============================

Reporting a bug: press F10 in the game. A drawer bugNNN appears in the save
directory (a text with the release number and the Amiga, the screen, the save,
the settings). With TCP/IP running the game pauses and opens a report window
(e-mail and your words, Enter on an empty line or F10 sends, Esc does not). Without a network add what happened to bugNNN.txt and send the drawer.


0.51 (beta) - 25 November 2026
------------------------------

- Bug report with F10 in a window in the middle of the screen: the e-mail,
  your words, the sending and its result in one place; the game is paused
  while the window is open


0.50 (beta) - 25 November 2026
------------------------------

- Automap: its text (level, difficulty) drawn from a cache; walking in the
  dungeon with the map about 23-27 frames a second (Warp 1260, 68060)
- All timer.device resources are freed when the game quits


0.49 (beta) - 25 November 2026
------------------------------

- A fast automap (Tab): walking in the dungeon with the map about 22 frames
  a second, standing about 40 (Warp 1260, 68060); only what changed goes to
  the card
- The program is compiled for the 68060 (it still runs on a 68040)
- Bug report with F10: a drawer save/bugNNN with the screen, the save, the
  settings and a description of the Amiga; sent to the game server (e-mail
  and your words in the chat bar)
- "Game saved" goes away after a second


0.48 (beta) - 14 November 2026
------------------------------

- Diablo: Hellfire: with hellfire.mpq, hfmonk.mpq, hfmusic.mpq and
  hfvoice.mpq in DiabloWarp/data the game runs as Hellfire (the Monk, the
  new dungeons); a Hellfire icon of its own, the devilutionx icon starts
  plain Diablo
- Tool Type GAME=DIABLO|HELLFIRE
- Videos with sound; a faster video decoder (the Hellfire intro in its
  own time on a 68060)


0.47 (beta) - 4 November 2026
-----------------------------

The first public release.

- Diablo with the DevilutionX engine (1.6.0-dev) for AmigaOS 3 and the Warp
  cards: an own SDL2 layer, an 8-bit Picasso96 screen of 640x480
- Renderer for the 68060: only what changed is drawn and written to the card,
  the card's blitter scrolls; 46 frames per second in a dungeon fight,
  91 standing in Tristram (Warp 7060, 100 MHz)
- Sound on Paula: effects, music and speech
- Videos twice the size
- English, Polish and German; Polish speech with a converted pl.mpq
- "Light Steps" option for faster walking in the dungeon
- Workbench icon with Tool Types (DATADIR, SAVEDIR, LANGUAGE, SHOWFPS,
  SKIPINTRO, NOSOUND), stack of 1000000 bytes
- Bug report on F10, crash details in RAM:Diablo-crash.txt
