DiabloWarp - release history
============================

Reporting a bug: press F10 in the game and send the files from
RAM:Diablo-report (the save, diablo.ini and a text with the version) with a
description of what happened and the version number (bottom left of the main
menu, or "Version devilutionx" in the Shell).


0.47 (beta) - 4 November 2026
-----------------------------

The first public release.

- Diablo with the DevilutionX engine (1.6.0-dev) for AmigaOS 3 and the Warp
  cards: an own SDL2 layer, an 8-bit Picasso96 screen of 640x480
- Renderer for the 68060: only what changed is drawn and written to the card,
  the card's blitter scrolls; 46 frames per second in a dungeon fight,
  91 standing in Tristram (Warp 7060, 100 MHz)
- Sound on Paula: effects, music and speech
- Videos twice the size
- English, Polish and German; Polish speech with a converted pl.mpq
- "Light Steps" option for faster walking in the dungeon
- Workbench icon with Tool Types (DATADIR, SAVEDIR, LANGUAGE, SHOWFPS,
  SKIPINTRO, NOSOUND), stack of 1000000 bytes
- Bug report on F10, crash details in RAM:Diablo-crash.txt
